## Publication standard

Every artifact should make four things easy to identify:

**Scope** — what was and was not assessed.  
**Evidence** — what supports the conclusion.  
**Limitations** — what the evidence cannot prove.  
**Decision** — what should happen next and under whose authority.

For confidentiality rules, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
