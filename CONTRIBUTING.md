# Contributing

This repository is a personal cybersecurity engineering portfolio, but corrections and constructive technical feedback are welcome.

## Principles

Contributions should preserve:

- evidence-driven language;
- explicit scope and limitations;
- reproducibility where practical;
- safe, non-destructive examples;
- confidentiality and responsible disclosure;
- separation between laboratory results and production claims.

## Do not submit

Do not open a contribution containing credentials, private logs, internal tickets, non-public vulnerabilities, proprietary source code, customer information or infrastructure identifiers.

## Technical corrections

When proposing a correction, explain:

1. the statement or behavior being corrected;
2. why it is inaccurate or ambiguous;
3. the proposed change;
4. a public authoritative reference when applicable.

## Security issues

Do not use a public issue to disclose a real vulnerability affecting a third party. Follow the affected project's responsible disclosure process instead.
