# Publication & Disclosure Gate — Operation Cymbal: Cold Room

**Status:** HOLD for challenge-specific solution disclosure.  
**Purpose:** Protect competition fairness, third-party material and user-held private evidence.

## Allowed public scope (current)

- Event identity and officially public organizer names
- Platform-observed completion totals with explicit limits
- Generic skills, source categories and analytical methodology
- Defensive lessons that do not disclose or reconstruct any challenge solution
- Honest assistance and affiliation statements

## Withheld from this public repository

- All answers, flags and challenge-specific hints
- Source/raw logs, timestamps and timelines that give away a challenge route
- Credentials, API keys, session data and internal identifiers
- Relevant attacker or victim IPs, domains, filenames and usernames
- Per-flag queries, event extracts, exploit instructions and private report
- Screen captures with the above content, whether direct or indirectly readable

## Required manual review before any expanded publication

1. **Confirm the event is over** and verify the applicable HTB/event disclosure terms. The end date alone does not grant publication rights.
2. **Obtain explicit authorization where required** from the content owner/organizer; do not infer authorization from rankings or certificate availability.
3. **Assess assistance rules** separately from platform completion and do not represent a one-person team as an unassisted solve.
4. **Inspect every proposed file** for secrets, unique challenge content, third-party material or reconstructable attack paths.
5. **Review provenance and accuracy:** differentiate direct observation, corroboration, inference and unsupported attribution.
6. **Use a branch and PR**; check link validation and secret scanning; require deliberate human review.
7. **Keep the private PDF outside the public repository** unless disclosure rights have been affirmatively verified and its full contents separately cleared for release.

## Post-event reminder

The repository's one-time scheduled GitHub Actions reminder for **2026-11-02** opens a **generic public issue** asking for this review. It **never changes visibility, publishes solutions or uploads the private report**.

If authorization remains unclear, keep this page and the case study spoiler-free, close the reminder issue with a note that the embargo remains in force, and recheck later.
