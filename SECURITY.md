# Security & Confidentiality Policy

This public portfolio follows a publication-safety standard.

## Never publish

Employer or client identities without authorization; production identifiers; real service-account identities; credentials, tokens, keys or secrets; private endpoints or raw operational logs; internal ticket/workflow identifiers; proprietary source code; reconstructable private architecture; unresolved non-public findings.

## Safe transformation rule

Real-world experience may inform methodology, but public artifacts use **synthetic data, controlled laboratories or sufficiently abstract reference architectures**.

Sanitization must not fabricate professional experience. Labs are identified as labs and synthetic evidence is identified as synthetic evidence.

## Pre-publication gate

Before publication, verify scope and authorization, remove reconstructable identifiers, ensure claims match evidence, state limitations, identify synthetic material, and confirm that no secret or private finding is present.

When in doubt, do not publish the sensitive detail.
